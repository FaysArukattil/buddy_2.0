Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Data Safety Compliance • Published: November 2, 2026

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Data Safety requirements, this page describes how users can delete their Buddy account and all associated data stored in Google Firebase and on their local device.

Permanent and Irreversible

Deleting your account permanently wipes your user profile, all parsed transactions, custom categories, and debt tracking records from Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication. This operation cannot be reversed.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Immediate)

You can execute complete account deletion at any time directly within the Buddy application:

Step 1: Open Settings Navigate to the Profile tab (or tap the Settings icon on the Home screen).
Step 2: Tap "Delete account" Under the Account section, select Delete account.
Step 3: Confirm Deletion Review the initial confirmation dialog and select Delete. In the final confirmation prompt, type the word DELETE and tap Delete Permanently.

Backend Actions Executed:

  • All transaction documents in Firestore (/users/{uid}/transactions) are deleted in batches.
  • All category documents in Firestore (/users/{uid}/categories) are deleted.
  • All debt and ledger documents in Firestore (/users/{uid}/debts) are deleted.
  • Your primary user profile document in Firestore (/users/{uid}) is deleted.
  • Your user record in Firebase Authentication is permanently deleted.
  • Google Sign-In session is disconnected.
  • All local cache, settings, and SharedPreferences on your device are wiped.
  • The app routes immediately to the Login screen with an "Account deleted successfully" confirmation.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can submit a deletion request directly to the developer:

  • Send an email to: faysarukattil@gmail.com
  • Subject: Buddy Account Deletion Request
  • Specify the Google Account email address linked to your Buddy account.

Turnaround: Your request will be verified and processed within 48 hours. An email confirmation will be sent once your records have been completely purged from Firebase.

Data Retention Policy

Buddy does not retain any archival copies of your transactions or account credentials following deletion. All records are permanently purged from Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication.

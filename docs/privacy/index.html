Privacy Policy

How Buddy protects your privacy through on-device parsing, zero bank logins, and isolated cloud sync.

Last Updated: November 2, 2026 • Effective Date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Buddy ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Fays Arukattil, handles your information across our mobile application and related web pages.

Overview of Architecture & Data Handling

Buddy uses on-device parsing to detect bank debited and credited notifications. Once parsed locally, the extracted transaction records (amounts, categories, debts) are stored and synced to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) for authenticated users to enable cloud backup and cross-device access. Raw notification message text is not uploaded to our servers.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Handled

A. Bank Transaction Detection (On-Device Parsing)

Buddy requests the BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE permission on Android to inspect financial notifications issued by your banking and payment apps:

  • On-Device Extraction: Our parsing engine operates locally in device memory to extract transaction details: the numerical amount, currency code, merchant or payee name, date, and transaction type (Debit/Credit).
  • No Raw Text Forwarding: The raw text body of your notifications and personal SMS messages is never transmitted to, collected by, or stored on external servers.
  • Exclusion of Non-Financial Content: One-time passwords (OTPs), verification codes, personal chat messages, and non-financial notifications are filtered out and ignored.

B. Data Stored in Google Firebase (After Parsing)

For users who choose to sign in (via Google Sign-In or Email/Password), Buddy utilizes Google Cloud Firebase infrastructure for authentication and data storage:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages your login identity, storing your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID). If signing in with Google, public avatar URLs provided by Google are also stored.
  • Cloud Firestore: The parsed transaction details (amount, category, merchant name, timestamp), user-defined categories, and borrow/lend (debts) records are synced to Cloud Firestore.
  • User Isolation: Firestore data is secured using server-side security rules ensuring that only your authenticated account (request.auth.uid == userId) has read or write access to your documents and subcollections.

C. Guest Mode (Offline Storage)

You may use Buddy without creating an account or signing in. In Guest Mode:

  • All parsed transactions, categories, and debts are stored exclusively on your device in local storage (SQLite/SharedPreferences).
  • No financial data is synced to Firebase until you explicitly choose to link or save your guest account.

2. Android System Permissions

Buddy utilizes the following permissions strictly to provide functionality:

  • BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE: To listen for and locally parse bank transaction notifications.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display transaction confirmations and reminders on Android 13+.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To automatically resume local parsing service after your phone restarts.
  • INTERNET: Required for Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore synchronization, and update checks.

3. Third-Party Services

Buddy relies on standard Google infrastructure services:

  • Google Play Services / Google Sign-In: Handles OAuth 2.0 authentication.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Manages user accounts and structured cloud persistence.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects non-identifiable app performance statistics and crash diagnostics to help us improve stability.

4. Open-Source Project Status

Buddy is currently maintained as an open-source project hosted on GitHub (https://github.com/FaysArukattil/buddy_2.0). The public availability of the source code allows users and researchers to review the on-device parsing implementation and security structure.

Notice of Potential Future Changes: Please note that project architecture, licensing terms, backend services, or open-source status may be updated, amended, or revised in future releases as the project develops. Any substantial modifications will be reflected in an updated version of this policy.

5. User Rights, Data Control, and Account Deletion

You maintain full authority over your data stored in Buddy and Firebase:

  • In-App Immediate Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, all cloud transaction documents, category records, debt ledgers, and local preferences directly in the app at Settings > Account > Delete account (requires typing "DELETE" to confirm). This permanently purges your data from Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore immediately.
  • Web / Email Requests: If you have uninstalled the application, you can submit a deletion request via our Account Deletion Page or by emailing faysarukattil@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 48 hours.

6. Security and Data Protection

Data transmitted between the mobile application and Firebase servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). Access to Firestore database collections is restricted by authenticated security rules. However, no electronic transmission or cloud storage system is completely infallible, and users should safeguard their Google account credentials.

7. Children's Privacy

Buddy is not directed to individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

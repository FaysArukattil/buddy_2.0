How Buddy Processes Your Data

A transparent look at the exact data lifecycle inside the application.

1

Bank Alert Arrives

When your bank app sends a transaction push notification or SMS alert, Android delivers the event to Buddy's native background listener.

2

On-Device Parsing

The parser runs locally in your device's memory. It extracts the transaction amount, merchant name, and date. Raw notification texts and SMS messages are not uploaded to our servers.

3

Firebase Cloud Storage

Once parsed, the structured ledger entry (amount, category, timestamp) is saved locally and synced to your private Google Cloud Firebase Firestore account (if signed in) for cloud backup.

4

User Isolation & Wipe

All Firebase data is protected by user-isolated security rules (request.auth.uid == userId). You can permanently delete your account and all cloud records in 1 tap.

Public, Open-Source Transparency

Buddy is currently an open-source project. Our codebase is publicly accessible on GitHub, allowing developers to inspect our parsing rules and backend integration. Please note that while the project is open source today, features, architecture, and terms may evolve in future updates.

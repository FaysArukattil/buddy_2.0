How Buddy Processes Your Data

A transparent look at the exact data lifecycle inside the application.

1

Bank Alert Arrives

When your bank app sends a transaction push notification or SMS alert, Android delivers the event to Buddy's native background listener.

2

On-Device Parsing

The parser runs locally in your device's memory. It extracts the transaction amount, merchant name, and date. Raw texts and OTPs are ignored.

3

Private Firebase Sync

Once parsed, structured ledger entries are stored locally and backed up to your private Google Cloud Firebase account if signed in.

4

User Isolation & Wipe

All data is protected by user-isolated security rules (request.auth.uid == userId). Delete your account and cloud data anytime in 1 tap.
